Welcome to Canvas Senior Secondary School

Located in Tarajan, Jorhat, Canvas Senior Secondary School is dedicated to nurturing academic excellence, fostering critical thinking, and shaping well-rounded leaders of tomorrow. Situated in the cultural heartland of Assam, Canvas Senior Secondary School provides a supportive, modern, and engaging educational environment where every student is empowered to discover their potential and achieve their dreams.

Address: Phukan-Ali, Tarajan, Jorhat, Assam, 785001

Academic Streams Offered

We offer comprehensive Higher Secondary programs across Science, Arts, and Commerce, designed to build a strong foundation for higher education and competitive examinations.

1. Science Stream

Our Science curriculum emphasizes conceptual clarity, practical application, and analytical thinking. Guided by experienced faculty, students are prepared for both board examinations and competitive entrance tests in engineering, medicine, research, and technology.

Key Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science/IP.

2. Commerce Stream

Designed for future entrepreneurs, managers, and financial experts, our Commerce program equips students with strong analytical skills in accounting, business dynamics, and economics.

Key Subjects: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Finance, Mathematics.

3. Arts (Humanities) Stream

Our Arts program encourages creative expression, critical analysis, and a deep understanding of human society and culture. It serves as an ideal launchpad for careers in civil services, law, journalism, literature, and social sciences.

Key Subjects: Political Science, History, Economics, Sociology, Geography, Advanced Languages, Logic & Philosophy.
